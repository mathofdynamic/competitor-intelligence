# Research orchestration

Normalize the brief before discovery. `profile_provenance.<field>` contains `origin`, `source_ids`, `notes`; origin is `USER PROVIDED`, `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED` or `UNKNOWN`. Record mixed per-item provenance in notes or separate findings. Unknown scalars are null; unknown lists are empty and explained in `unknowns`. Inferred scope is not an observed chart fact.

Defaults without a budget: up to 20 credible research subjects, 12 relevant pages per subject, 90 minutes overall. Adjust to the brief/capacity. Record deferred subjects, time/page limits and dimension gaps. A budget stop is not saturation. Plan industry extensions before assignments.

Unknown robots/terms means defer collection. Search results can establish candidates without company facts. Preserve blocked source records; never bypass restrictions through proxies, another identity or hidden endpoints. Default automated collection: one request per host per two seconds, one in flight per host; honor stricter guidance and Retry-After. Aggregate rates across workers and shared corporate domains.

Focal-company sources/findings use `competitor_id = company_id`, otherwise identical schemas/dimensions. User-provided facts live in profile provenance or tagged comparison cells; do not invent public sources for them. Treat focal and competitor limitations equally.

The main agent checks material findings against source passages and completes all reports. Unsupported dimensions have empty data with explicit limitations. Record stale policies, inaccessible links, uncertain prices and unanswered goals. Structural validity cannot prove factual completeness.
