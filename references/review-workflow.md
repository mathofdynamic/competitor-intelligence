# Package v1 review boundary and QC

All generated findings remain needs_review. Import readiness permits review ingestion, not publication or factual approval. Human/application review occurs outside this skill; no named reviewer or approval is required to start research. AI cannot approve findings.

Main-agent QC verifies public identity/domain, citation accessibility or limitations, passages, dates, primary/third-party attribution, normalization, aliases, duplicate claims, conflicts and comparable dimensions. A worker source list/confidence is insufficient for material claims. Remove unsupported findings and record why; retain unknowns and conflicts.

Opportunities link findings, rationale, company provenance, alternatives and uncertainty. Rare observed capabilities do not establish demand or commercial impact. The receiving reviewer applies its own decision policy and preserves observations/revisions.

The following historical application review workflow applies only after import to a separately authorized receiving application. Its approval states must never be generated by this skill.

# Legacy human review workflow

## Queue states

`candidate` and `unverified` sources require source review. `needs_review` findings require evidence and interpretation review. `conflict` findings require a conflict note and a decision about scope or recency. `stale` findings require re-observation before reuse.

## Reviewer checklist

- Is the source public, allowed and relevant to the declared market?
- Does the evidence manifest point to the exact source and observation time?
- Does the observed text support the normalized value without an unsupported conversion?
- Are units, currency, tax, availability and localization clear?
- Are interpretation and limitations visibly separate from observation?
- Does another finding conflict by product, date, unit or scope?
- Is the decision made by a named human with a review note?

The reviewer may approve, reject, mark stale, or leave the finding in review. A reviewer cannot approve their own unreviewed source without a separate source-review policy decision. AI may suggest extraction or conflicts, but it cannot approve, publish, change source status or perform consequential mutations.

Every decision creates a new revision record or append-only event. Never delete the original observation to make a report look consistent.
